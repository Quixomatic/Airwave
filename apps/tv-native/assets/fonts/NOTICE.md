For three families (Fraunces, IBM Plex Sans Condensed and Source Sans 3) the font files carry a later year in their own name table, as their projects released them; that notice is given in brackets, and both are kept as the projects have them.

The fonts are not sold on their own. Reserved Font Names: "Plex" (IBM Plex Sans Condensed) and "Source" (Source Sans 3); those two families are bundled unmodified. So are the others, which are their projects' static files (from google/fonts, or the project's own repository), except Fraunces: its two faces, Bold and SemiBold Italic, are instanced from the variable font at an optical size of 24 with SOFT and WONK at 0, and named plainly "Fraunces".
