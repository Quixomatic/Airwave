Airwave turns the media you already own into real television. Point it at your Plex library and it builds persistent, scheduled channels with a full program guide — like a classic cable box, but generated entirely from your own library and running on your own hardware.

This app is the Roku client for a self-hosted Airwave server. The server owns your channels, schedules, packages, and users; every client — Roku, Apple TV, Android TV, Fire TV, LG webOS, iPad, and the browser — tunes into the same network. Same channels, same guide, same program at the same point in its schedule, on every screen.

Build channels from metadata filters, Plex collections, playlists, or hand-picked media. A deterministic scheduling engine groups episodes by show, rotates between shows, builds marathons, and enforces no-repeat windows. When you tune in, playback starts at the current live moment — not the beginning of the file.

Airwave keeps each channel's past as a DVR: seek backward across program boundaries, restart the current program, or jump into something that aired earlier and keep watching forward. LIVE is the upper bound — go back through the channel, never past what's airing now. Optional bumpers and ambient music round out the experience.

Requires a self-hosted Airwave server connected to Plex. The server, admin, and browser player are free and source-available to self-host; this prebuilt Roku app is a small paid convenience.
