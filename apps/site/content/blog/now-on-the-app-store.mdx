Quick but exciting update: **Airwave is now live on the Apple TV App Store.**

You can search for it right on your Apple TV, or [grab it here](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6801608850). Install
it, point it at your Airwave server, sign in with Plex, and you land straight in the guide. No sideloading, no
developer mode, no TestFlight. Just a normal app from the store.

## Desktop is ready too

Alongside the Apple TV launch, the **native Windows and macOS desktop apps** are available to download now from
[getairwave.tv](https://getairwave.tv). These aren't a browser tab in a wrapper. They run a real mpv engine, so
they direct-play the same 4K, HDR, and lossless-audio content the TV apps do, with the full guide and DVR.

## The rest of the platforms are on their way

This is the start of a wider rollout, not the finish line. Every store has its own review process, and Airwave is
working through each one:

- **iPad** shares the same universal App Store listing as Apple TV and is in review.
- **Android TV and Fire TV** are in their testing and review tracks.
- **Roku** is submitted and on its way.
- **LG webOS** can be sideloaded today, and the browser player works everywhere in the meantime.

I'll post here as each one goes live.

## And it stays free to self-host

None of this changes the core promise. The server, the admin, and the browser player are free and source-available
to run yourself. The native store apps are a small paid convenience for people who would rather install from a store
than sideload. Your library, your server, your channels.
