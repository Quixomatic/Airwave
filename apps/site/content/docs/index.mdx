**Airwave** is a self-hostable service that turns your Plex library into custom, always-on **live-TV
channels** — a real channel guide, DVR, and bumpers — streamed directly from your own Plex to native
apps on webOS, Apple TV, iPad, Android TV, and Fire TV. It's free and self-hosted: you run the server,
there's no subscription.

You build channels from **metadata filters** over your library ("90s comedies", "everything Studio
Ghibli", "unwatched sci-fi"), Airwave lays each one onto a continuous, deterministic timeline, and
your viewers channel-surf it like cable — joining whatever's on *now*, mid-program, at the correct
offset.
