Welcome to the **Airwave** documentation.

Airwave is a self-hostable service that turns your Plex library into custom, always-on **live-TV
channels** — a real channel guide, DVR, and bumpers, streamed directly from your own Plex to native
apps on webOS, Apple TV, iPad, Android TV, and Fire TV.

> This documentation is a work in progress. Pages here are scaffolding — the real guides (install,
> channel building, self-hosting, clients) land as we build them out from the existing `docs/` guides.

## Sections (coming soon)

- **Getting started** — stand up the server, connect Plex, build your first channels.
- **Channels** — the flexible channel model, ordering, strategies, and bumpers.
- **Clients** — webOS, Apple TV, iPad, Android TV, Fire TV.
- **Self-hosting** — Docker, TrueNAS, remote playback.
