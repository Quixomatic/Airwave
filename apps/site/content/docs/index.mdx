You know the feeling — you turn on the TV, something's already playing, and you drop into a movie halfway
through instead of staring at a catalog deciding what to watch. **Airwave** brings that back, built from
media you already own.

Airwave is a **self-hostable service that turns your Plex library into custom, always-on live-TV
channels** — a real channel guide, DVR, and bumpers — streamed straight from your own Plex to native apps
on LG webOS, Apple TV, iPad, Android TV, and Fire TV, plus any browser. It's free and self-hosted: you run
the server, and there's no subscription.

Make a 90s-sitcom channel, a Saturday-morning-cartoons channel, a kids' channel, or one that quietly
marathons a favorite show — from practically any mix of genre, year, network, cast, rating, collection, or
playlist. Airwave turns that into a continuous schedule and presents it through a proper 10-foot guide.

## What makes it different

- **You join what's airing.** Tune in and you land on whatever's on *now*, at the point it would actually
  be — not the start of a file. Everyone watching a channel sees the same thing at the same moment.
- **Live, but with a DVR.** Scrub back through programs that already aired, restart what's on, or jump to
  an earlier episode. The only thing you can't do is seek *ahead* of live.
- **The channels belong to your server, not a device.** The server owns the lineup, schedules, packages,
  users, and playback state; every app is just a client tuning into the same network. Open Airwave
  anywhere and you see the same channels and the same thing currently airing.

The result is discovery — you stumble onto something buried in your library and end up watching it,
instead of endlessly scrolling thumbnails. For the fuller story of why Airwave exists, see
[About Airwave](/about).
