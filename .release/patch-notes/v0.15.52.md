## New Features

- **Library Health: auto-disable a deleted Plex library, and bring it back if it returns** (issue #36). A new job runs every 15 minutes and cheaply checks that each source's libraries still exist on the server. A library that has vanished (for example, deleted in Plex) is auto-disabled after three consecutive failed checks, so metadata sync, library scans, and schedule builds stop erroring on a section that no longer exists. If it reappears it is turned back on automatically. The Sources page shows a per-library health badge (Healthy, Missing n/3, Not found). A library you disable by hand stays disabled: the job only manages libraries it disabled itself. (0.15.37-0.15.40)

- **Custom request headers for OpenAI-compatible AI connections** (issue #58). Settings, AI Assistant, a compatible connection, Advanced now has an "Extra request headers" editor with add/remove key-value rows. The headers are sent on every request to that endpoint, including the connection Test, so endpoints that require a header can finally be used (the motivating case is OpenCode Go, which rejects any request missing `x-opencode-session`; a custom `User-Agent` is another). Headers are stored encrypted at rest, the same as the API key. Reserved transport and auth headers (Authorization, Content-Type, Host, and the like) cannot be overridden. (0.15.41)

- **Keep multi-part episodes together** (issue #37). A per-channel toggle in the channel editor's Options. When on, consecutive episodes that form one split story (titles like "... (1) / (2)", "... Part 2", "... (II)") are scheduled contiguously and in order wherever they land, no matter the ordering or grouping strategy. Detection is title-based, the only signal the media server exposes: a run must start at part 1 and is capped at 5 parts. Interstitial bumpers are still woven between the parts. Off by default, and when off the schedule is unchanged. The AI assistant can see the setting when describing a channel. (0.15.43-0.15.45)

- **Exclude TV specials from a channel** (issue #7). An "Exclude specials" toggle at the top of Advanced grouping and rotation. When on, it drops Season 0 episodes from the channel's pool so specials never appear in the schedule, across all modes (filter, membership, manual). Movies and normal seasons are unaffected. Off by default. (0.15.46-0.15.47)

- **A richer channel editor: a floating section nav and a redesigned Schedule panel.** (0.15.48-0.15.52)
  - A floating "On this page" section nav appears on wide screens (1800px and up): the channel as a parent with its sections nested beneath, plus Preview and Schedule, with a moving rail that highlights every section in view and click-to-scroll. It overlays without affecting the form's width, and hides when there isn't room or when the AI assistant panel is open.
  - The Schedule panel's "on now" block now reads like an active session in Settings, Sessions: a portrait poster in a left gutter, the title and metadata beside it, and a progress band (a thin bar with elapsed and total time plus a Live marker). "Up next" and "Lineup runs until" sit in a recessed footer strip.
  - The upcoming lineup is now a vertical timeline: a timestamp in a left gutter, an outline dot and continuous rail down the middle, and the program title, badges (season and episode, resolution, audio, content rating), and duration to the right. The program on now is the active step, so its dot shows in the live red and its title is bold. Interstitial breaks pass through the rail without a dot and show their exact length in seconds.

## Improvements

- **The channel scheduling toggles read better.** The switches in Advanced grouping and rotation (Exclude specials, Keep multi-part together, Group and rotate) put the switch in a left gutter with the label and a width-limited description beside it, instead of a full-width blurb under each. (0.15.48)

## Fixes

- **The "Keep multi-part episodes together" toggle now saves and loads correctly.** It previously always showed off and a change to it was dropped on save, because the channel edit and clone pages built their form values and save payload by cherry-picking fields and the new toggle was in neither list. (0.15.45)

## Under the hood

- A reusable `Timeline` component now lives in the shared UI package, and the channel editor's Schedule panel was split into focused components (`ChannelNowPlaying`, `ChannelScheduleTimeline`) over a shared guide-metadata helper. The floating section nav is a reusable `toc` component. (0.15.49-0.15.51)
- The scheduler internals for multi-part grouping (`timeline.ts`) are wired through generate, extend, and repair, and covered by tests. (0.15.43)
- Database migrations for the release are all additive and defaulted (no-ops for existing data): library-health fields, the AI-connection encrypted-headers column, the keep-multi-part flag, and the exclude-specials flag.

## Note
